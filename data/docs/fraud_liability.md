# Fraud Liability

If the cardholder reports an unauthorised transaction within 3 days, the cardholder has zero liability for that transaction.

If the unauthorised transaction is reported between 4 and 7 days, liability is limited to a maximum of INR 10,000.

Lost or stolen cards must be blocked within 24 hours of discovery using the mobile app or the helpline. Reports after 7 days are handled under case-by-case review by the fraud team.
