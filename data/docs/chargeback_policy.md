# Chargeback and Dispute Policy

The dispute window is 120 days. Cardholders have 120 days from the transaction date to raise a chargeback dispute with NovaPay.

After a dispute is opened, the cardholder must submit supporting documents within 10 business days. Disputes without documents within this period are closed automatically.

NovaPay issues a provisional credit within 5 business days for disputes involving card-present fraud. The provisional credit is reversed if the dispute is decided in favour of the merchant.

The final dispute decision is communicated within 45 days of the dispute being opened.
